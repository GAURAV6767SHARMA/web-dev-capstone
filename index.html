FAQ

Good questions, clear answers.

Still curious? Send us an enquiry.

Is Razor free to use?

Yes. The Basic plan is free and includes transaction tracking, monthly summaries, and the standard dashboard.

How secure are transactions?

Payment flows are designed around encryption, and every transaction is shown clearly so unusual activity is easy to spot.

Can I track monthly expenses?

Yes. Monthly summaries group your spending by category so you can see where your money goes each month.

Does Razor support UPI?

Yes. UPI is listed alongside Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, and PayPal among the supported payment methods.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes. You can move from Basic to Pro or Business whenever your needs change.
